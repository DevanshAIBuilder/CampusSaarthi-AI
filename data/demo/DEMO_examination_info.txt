Integral University, Lucknow (Illustrative – Hacktoberfest Demo 2026)

SECTION 1: END-SEMESTER EXAMINATION SCHEDULE

The end-semester examinations for November–December 2026 are scheduled as follows:

- Theory examinations: 20 November 2026 to 10 December 2026
- Practical examinations: 11 December 2026 to 20 December 2026
- Supplementary examinations: January 2027 (dates to be announced)

Note: The exact date-sheet will be published on the university portal at least 21 days before the first paper.

SECTION 2: EXAMINATION ELIGIBILITY

Students must fulfill the following conditions to appear in end-semester examinations:
1. Minimum 75% attendance in each subject.
2. Submission of all assignments and practicals.
3. No outstanding dues (fee arrears) at the time of examination.
4. A valid examination admit card, downloadable from the student portal.

Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may apply for a condonation from the Dean (Academic Affairs) by 10 November 2026. Applications are not guaranteed approval.

SECTION 3: EXAMINATION FEES

Examination fees are included in the regular semester fee. No separate examination fee is charged for regular students.

For re-appear (back paper) examinations:
- Per paper re-appear fee: Rs. 500 (DEMO AMOUNT – not official)
- Application deadline for back papers: Within 7 days of results being declared

SECTION 4: ADMIT CARD PROCEDURE

1. Log in to the student portal at https://portal.iul.ac.in (illustrative URL).
2. Navigate to "Examinations" → "Admit Card".
3. Download and print the admit card.
4. Show the admit card along with a valid university identity card at the examination hall.

Students who cannot access the portal should contact the Examination Department at Block A, Room 101.

SECTION 5: RULES DURING EXAMINATION

- No electronic devices are permitted in the examination hall.
- Students must arrive at least 15 minutes before the scheduled start time.
- No entry is permitted after 30 minutes from the start of the examination.
- Answer sheets must be written in blue or black ball-point pen only.

SECTION 6: RESULT DECLARATION

Results are typically declared within 45 days of the last examination. They are published on the university portal and the official notice board.

Re-checking and re-evaluation:
- Re-checking (photocopy of answer sheet): Rs. 200 per paper (DEMO AMOUNT)
- Re-evaluation: Rs. 500 per paper (DEMO AMOUNT)
- Deadline: 15 days from the date of result declaration
