SECTION 3: PAYMENT METHODS

Fees can be paid through the following channels:
1. Online payment via the student portal (https://portal.iul.ac.in) using:
   - Net banking
   - UPI
   - Debit card / Credit card
2. Demand draft in favour of "Integral University" payable at Lucknow.
3. Cash payment at the Finance Office (Block B, Ground Floor) during working hours.
