DEMO: STUDENT REGISTRATION AND ENROLLMENT PROCEDURES
Integral University, Lucknow (Illustrative – Hacktoberfest Demo 2026)
================================================================================

SECTION 1: FRESH ADMISSION REGISTRATION

New students admitted for the academic year 2026–27 must complete registration within 7 days of receiving their admission letter.

Required documents for registration:
1. Original Class X certificate and mark-sheet
2. Original Class XII certificate and mark-sheet
3. Transfer Certificate (TC) from the last institution
4. Migration Certificate (for students from other universities)
5. Six passport-size photographs (colour, white background)
6. Character Certificate from the last institution
7. Domicile Certificate (if applicable)
8. Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC) issued by competent authority (if applicable)
9. Medical fitness certificate from a registered medical practitioner

SECTION 2: SEMESTER REGISTRATION (CONTINUING STUDENTS)

All continuing students must register at the start of each semester.

Registration period for Odd Semester 2026–27:
- Online registration window: 1 August 2026 – 14 August 2026 (DEMO DATES)
- Fee payment deadline: 15 August 2026 (DEMO DATE)
- Late registration (with late fee of Rs. 200): 16 August 2026 – 22 August 2026

Steps for semester registration:
1. Log in to the student portal.
2. Navigate to "Registration" → "Semester Registration".
3. Verify enrolled subjects and electives.
4. Confirm subject selection.
5. Pay the semester fee online or submit the fee receipt at the Finance Office.
6. Download the registration confirmation slip.

SECTION 3: COURSE ADD/DROP

Students may add or drop elective courses during the first week of the semester.
- Add/drop window: 18 August 2026 – 25 August 2026 (DEMO DATES)
- Approval required from: Faculty Advisor and Head of Department
- Form available at: Academic Section, Block A

Core/mandatory courses cannot be dropped without special permission from the Dean.

SECTION 4: RE-ADMISSION AFTER GAP YEAR

Students seeking re-admission after a gap must:
1. Submit an application to the Registrar within 30 days of the semester start.
2. Provide a valid reason for the gap with supporting documents.
3. Pay a re-admission fee of Rs. 1,000 (DEMO AMOUNT).
4. Re-admission is subject to approval and seat availability.
